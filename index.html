Official Rules

QUARTS (QUALITY DOODLES)

Objective: Draw as many called-out items as you can in 30 seconds, then name them clearly to get your game masters' approval for points.

Mechanics - The Sprint: 30 Seconds

  • Start Timer: Set a 30-second clock.
  • Call & Draw: Game masters call out everyday objects one at a time (max 9). The player sketches each as fast as possible.
  • Time's up: Release the mouse/touch immediately when time expires.

Show & Tell: Verification

  • Present: Display the monitor to the game masters.
  • Identify: Point to each drawing on the screen and state in any order what it is.
  • Approve: Game masters will agree if the drawing clearly matches the answer.

Scoring

  • Clear Drawing: 1 pt per approved drawing (minimum of 5 points will be awarded one (1) giveaway).
  • Full Sweep: Two (2) giveaways if all 9 items are drawn and approved.
Note: Every participant will receive a QuARTS sticker automatically.
