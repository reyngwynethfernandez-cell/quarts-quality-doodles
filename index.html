ETE QUALITY WORKSTREAM

QUARTS

(QUALITY DOODLES)

"Speed, accuracy, and clear visual communication under 30 seconds."

Game Objective

Draw as many called-out items as you can in 30 seconds, then name them clearly to get your game masters' approval for points.

Game Mechanics

The Sprint: 30 Seconds
  • 1
    Start timer: Set a 30-second clock.
  • 2
    Call & Draw: Game masters call out everyday objects one at a time (max 9). The player sketches each as fast as possible.
  • 3
    Time's up: Release the mouse/pen immediately when time expires.

Show & Tell: Verification

Present

Display the monitor to the game masters clearly after the 30-second sprint ends.

Identify

Point to each drawing on the screen and state in any order what it is.

Approve

Game masters will agree if the drawing clearly matches the answer.

Scoring & Rewards

Clear Drawing 1 pt / approved

Minimum of 5 points will be awarded one (1) giveaway.

Full Sweep 9 / 9 Perfect

Win two (2) giveaways if all 9 items are drawn and approved!

Automatic Participation Reward: Every participant will receive a QuARTS sticker automatically!
